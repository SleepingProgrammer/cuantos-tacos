Chef Luis "Beto" Robledo

Since 2019, one pan at a time.

Since September 2019, chef and owner Luis "Beto" Robledo has been serving Mexico City–style street tacos and quesadillas out of a bright yellow truck in East Austin. The name asks the only question that matters at the counter: “¿Cuántos tacos?” — how many tacos?

Every meat is cooked in a traditional chorizera pan with a raised center, where cuts confit and sear in their own juices before being chopped to order. Everything lands on 100% nixtamal corn tortillas that are bright yellow and paper-thin, finished with chopped onion and cilantro.

The suadero (brisket) taco is the signature, with cachete (beef cheek) a close second and the adobo-marinated champiñones (mushroom) taco as the pick for anyone wanting less meat. In both 2024 and 2025, Cuantos Tacos was recognized with a Michelin Guide Bib Gourmand.
